ABOUT ME

Understanding ecological change through research and data.

I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Plant Science and Landscape Architecture at Hankyong National University, Republic of Korea. My academic background includes biodiversity and environmental management, ecology, conservation biology, remote sensing, and GIS.

My research focuses particularly on the effects of climate and environmental change on biodiversity. I am interested in invasive species, freshwater ecosystems, waterbirds, habitat suitability, and the application of species distribution models to ecological and conservation questions.
